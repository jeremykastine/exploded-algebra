Try Exploded Algebra

The best way to see what Exploded Algebra is all about is to jump in and try it. Choose a problem below to start in Unguided mode. To follow a recorded solution, open the workspace menu and choose Restart Guided.

When you see a dotted yellow outline, select the part inside it by dragging across it or tapping multiple times until the whole part is selected. Press the button highlighted in yellow, and keep following the prompts until the exercise is complete. Use the workspace menu to restart in Unguided mode or close the problem and return here.
